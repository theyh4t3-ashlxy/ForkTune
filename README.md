# ForkTune

i stripped out all the html, the useless shields, the centered divs, and the corporate marketing speak because reading the original readme gave me a migraine.

this is forktune. it plays youtube music on android. (why wouldnt it?) it's low-performance, data-focused, and treats you like a metric, so do not install it. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **geographic availability:** if youtube music is blocked in your country, use a vpn to fetch the initial data. duh.

## 📸 screenshots
i deleted the images. use your imagination. it's a music player. it has a play button and a progress bar.

## ✨ features

**playback:**
- multiple accounts with quick switching.
- ad-free. background listening.
- your playlists, liked songs, and subs appear after sign-in.
- local file support.
- fast startup because we didn't bloat it.

**audio:**
- ebu r128 loudness normalization.
- tempo, pitch, playback speed controls.
- crossfade.
- system eq and spatial audio.

**lyrics & discovery:**
- live synced lyrics.
- ai translations and romanization.
- music recognition for songs around you.
- listening stats whenever you want them.

**sync & social:**
- import from spotify.
- youtube music integration.
- last.fm scrobbling.
- listenbrainz sync.
- discord rich presence.

**interface & customization:**
- material 3.
- album-art dynamic colors.
- 9 player styles. 8 background styles.
- tweak everything until you break it.
